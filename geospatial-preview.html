Project Concept & Scientific Methodology

When preparing for crewed deep-space missions, identifying geological look-alikes on Earth is vital for astronaut training and rover navigation testing. The Geospatial Analog Matcher utilizes NASA Open APIs and elevation data to cross-examine harsh terrestrial environments (such as the Atacama Desert or Icelandic volcanic fields) with Jezero Crater or Shackleton Crater coordinates.

Dataset Integration

Pulls real-time elevation vectors, slope gradients, and spectral reflectance indexes.

Mission Utility

Assists habitat site selection by evaluating terrain hazards and hazard visibility.
