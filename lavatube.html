Lunar / Martian Subsurface Lava Tube Mapper

Acoustic echo reflection and active seismic sounding matrix designed to map underground basaltic caverns for cosmic-shielded human habitats.

The Engineering Problem

Surface habitats on the Moon and Mars face lethal cosmic radiation, extreme thermal fluctuations (-150°C to +120°C), and micro-meteorite impacts. Deploying heavy shielding on the surface is structurally unfeasible and hazardous for crewed missions.

Our Prototyped Solution

Our active seismic reflection matrix identifies stable underground basaltic lava tubes. This provides natural subterranean shielding—blocking 99% of radiation and offering stable ambient temperatures for permanent human colonization.
